# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Public visitors who want to discover and evaluate independent software tools. They need a quick sense of what each tool does and a clear path to try it or inspect its source when those links are available.

## Product Purpose

A bilingual personal showcase for software tools built and completed by the owner. It helps visitors find relevant tools and open them. Product entries will be added by the owner after the initial frontend is built.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, precise, and software-minded. The experience should feel considered and trustworthy while keeping attention on the tools themselves.

## Anti-references

Avoid cluttered SaaS marketing patterns, decorative motion, invented product details, fabricated metrics, testimonials, and logos.

## Design Principles

- Put the tools and their purpose before promotional copy.
- Make each product easy to scan and reach.
- Keep Vietnamese and English content equally usable.
- Use motion only when it clarifies interaction.
- Keep the interface accessible and responsive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard accessibility. Respect reduced-motion preferences and support text resizing and narrow viewports.
